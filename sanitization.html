Air Duct Sanitization & Deodorizing in Orlando, FL

After a thorough air duct cleaning, your ductwork is free of visible debris — but microscopic contaminants can still cling to duct surfaces. Bacteria, viruses, mold spores, dust mite allergens, and volatile organic compounds (VOCs) remain on duct walls and can be redistributed into your living space over time as air flows through the system. Our sanitization service applies a hospital-grade, EPA-registered antimicrobial treatment throughout your entire duct system, creating a protective barrier that inhibits microbial growth for months after treatment.

In Orlando's humid climate, where conditions are perpetually favorable for mold and bacterial growth inside ductwork, sanitization is not a luxury — it's a smart preventive measure. Rather than waiting for mold to establish itself and require costly remediation, a routine sanitization treatment following each duct cleaning keeps your system biologically clean between service visits.

Our deodorizing treatment uses a professional-grade fogging process to neutralize odor-causing compounds at their source inside the ductwork. This is fundamentally different from air fresheners or deodorizing sprays, which only mask odors temporarily. Our fog reaches every inch of your duct system — including areas no brush or vacuum can access — and chemically neutralizes odor molecules rather than covering them. Pet odors, cigarette smoke, cooking smells, and musty moisture odors are all effectively eliminated, not masked.

  • Full duct system coverage via fog application

  • Inhibits mold and mildew regrowth
  • Neutralizes pet, smoke & cooking odors

  • Included in Standard & Premium packages

Sanitization Is Especially Recommended If You:

  • Have pets that shed or have had accidents
  • Recently moved into a previously occupied home
  • Have allergy or asthma sufferers in the household
  • Notice persistent musty or stale odors from vents
  • Have had a household member with a contagious illness
  • Want maximum protection between duct cleanings

Our sanitization products are certified by the EPA as safe for use in residential and commercial occupied spaces. After the fogging treatment, we recommend allowing the system to run for 30–60 minutes before full occupancy so the product can fully coat all duct surfaces and dry completely. The treated surfaces remain protected against microbial growth for several months, making this a highly cost-effective preventive treatment.

What Sanitization Eliminates

  • Bacteria and viruses on duct surfaces
  • Mold and mildew spores
  • Pet dander and odors
  • Cigarette and smoke odors
  • Cooking and food odors
  • Musty and moisture smells
  • Dust mite allergen residue
