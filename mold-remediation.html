Mold Remediation for Air Ducts in Orlando, FL

Orlando is one of the most challenging cities in the United States for mold growth. With average humidity levels regularly exceeding 70% and temperatures that stay warm year-round, our climate is essentially perfect for mold to establish itself inside air duct systems. When mold colonizes your ductwork, your HVAC system transforms from a comfort provider into a contamination distribution machine — spreading microscopic mold spores to every room in your home every time it turns on.

Mold inside ducts is frequently invisible and often goes undetected until occupants begin experiencing symptoms: chronic congestion, coughing, headaches, fatigue, skin irritation, or worsening asthma and allergies. A musty odor from your vents — especially when the AC first turns on — is one of the clearest indicators of mold growth inside the duct system. If you or your family members feel noticeably better when outside the home, mold contamination inside your HVAC system may be the cause.

Premier Air Duct Cleaning Orlando follows strict NADCA and EPA protocols for mold remediation in air duct systems. We begin with a thorough camera inspection to document the extent of contamination, then perform a full physical removal of all mold-contaminated debris before applying EPA-registered antimicrobial treatments to inhibit future growth. This is not a spray-and-go service — we physically remove the mold first, then treat the surfaces.

Our Mold Remediation Process:

  • Full camera inspection to identify mold location and extent
  • Containment setup to prevent cross-contamination to clean areas
  • Physical removal of all mold-contaminated material and debris
  • HEPA-filtered vacuum cleanup of all dislodged particles
  • Application of EPA-registered antimicrobial treatment to all duct surfaces
  • Antimicrobial fogging treatment throughout the entire duct system
  • Post-treatment inspection and documentation
  • Written recommendations to prevent future mold recurrence

Signs of Mold in Your Air Ducts:

  • Musty smell from vents when AC or heat turns on
  • Visible black, green, or white spots near vent registers
  • Chronic respiratory symptoms that improve when away from home
  • Unexplained headaches, fatigue, or skin irritation indoors
  • High indoor humidity levels (above 60% consistently)
  • History of water damage, flooding, or roof leaks

After remediation, we strongly recommend our sanitization service to apply a long-lasting antimicrobial protective coating throughout the duct system, which provides an additional barrier against future mold colonization. We also provide guidance on humidity control strategies to help prevent recurrence in Orlando's challenging climate.

Why Orlando Homes Are High Risk

  • Average humidity 70%+ year-round
  • AC condensation inside ducts
  • Warm temps accelerate mold growth
  • Year-round HVAC use = more moisture
  • Common in older Orlando homes
  • Spreads rapidly once established
