Our Story

Locally Owned & Operated in Orlando Since 2009

Premier Air Duct Cleaning Orlando was founded with a single mission: to give Orlando families access to genuinely clean, healthy indoor air. We saw too many homeowners being overcharged for rushed, low-quality duct cleaning and decided to do things differently.

From our very first job in 2009, we built our reputation on three principles — transparency, thoroughness, and trust. We show you exactly what's inside your ducts before we clean them, we don't leave until the job is done right, and we price our services fairly with zero hidden fees.

Today, we've served over 5,000 homes and businesses across Orlando and Central Florida. Every single member of our team is NADCA-certified, background-checked, and trained to treat your home with the same care and respect we would give our own. When you invite Premier Air Duct Cleaning into your home, you're getting the best in the business.

How It Works

Our Simple 4-Step Process

From the moment you contact us to the moment we leave your home, here's exactly what to expect.

1

Contact & Schedule

Call us at +1 321 732 6401 or fill out our online form. We confirm your appointment within 1 hour and work around your schedule — 24/7, including weekends.

2

Free Camera Inspection

Our NADCA-certified technician arrives on time and inspects your entire duct system with a camera — completely free. We show you exactly what's inside before we begin.

3

Professional Deep Cleaning

We connect our commercial-grade HEPA vacuum system and use agitation tools to dislodge and remove every particle from your ducts, from main trunks to every vent register.

4

Final Inspection & Report

After cleaning, we do a final camera walkthrough so you can see the results. We leave your home spotless and provide a complete written service report.
