Professional Air Duct Cleaning in Orlando, FL

Your home's ductwork is a hidden highway of dust, allergens, pet dander, mold spores, and debris that accumulates silently over years. Every time your HVAC system runs, it circulates these contaminants through every room in your home. In Orlando's warm, humid climate, this problem is accelerated — Florida's high humidity creates ideal conditions for mold and bacteria to thrive inside ductwork year-round.

At Premier Air Duct Cleaning Orlando, we use commercial-grade HEPA-filtered negative air machines combined with mechanical agitation tools to dislodge and capture every particle inside your duct system — from the main trunk lines down to every individual supply and return register. This is not a shop-vac job. Our equipment creates negative pressure throughout your duct system, preventing any dislodged particles from escaping back into your living space.

What's Included in Our Air Duct Cleaning Service:

  • Full camera inspection before cleaning
  • HEPA negative pressure vacuum system
  • Mechanical agitation of all ducts
  • All supply and return vents cleaned
  • Main trunk and branch duct cleaning
  • Register grilles removed and cleaned
  • Plenum box and air handler inspection
  • Post-cleaning camera walkthrough
  • Before & after video documentation
  • Written service report

Warning Signs You Need Air Duct Cleaning:

  • Dust blowing from vents when AC runs
  • Musty or stale odors coming from vents
  • Worsening allergy or asthma symptoms at home
  • Visible mold growth near or inside vents
  • Excessive household dust accumulation
  • Recent home renovation or construction work
  • More than 3 years since last professional cleaning

Florida homeowners should consider duct cleaning every 2–3 years rather than the national recommendation of 3–5 years, due to our persistently high humidity levels and year-round HVAC use. Homes with pets, allergy sufferers, or young children benefit most from more frequent service.

Our Process

How We Clean Your Air Ducts

1

Free Camera Inspection

We inspect your entire duct system with a camera and show you exactly what's inside before we begin any work.

2

Negative Pressure Setup

We connect our HEPA-filtered vacuum system to your main duct line, creating negative pressure throughout the entire system.

3

Agitation & Cleaning

Our technician works from each register back toward the vacuum, using rotary brushes and compressed air to dislodge all debris.

4

Final Inspection & Report

Post-cleaning camera walkthrough, full before/after video, and written service report provided before we leave.
