If you haven't had your air ducts professionally cleaned in the past three years, you're likely recirculating polluted air throughout your home every time your HVAC system runs.

At Premier Air Duct Cleaning Orlando, our residential air duct cleaning service is the most thorough in Central Florida. Our air duct cleaning service directly addresses this risk, leaving your duct system clean, dry, and healthy.

Dryer Vent Cleaning in Orlando, FL

Eliminate fire hazards & improve dryer performance with professional vent cleaning

A clogged dryer vent is one of the leading causes of house fires in the United States. According to the U.S. Fire Administration, nearly 3,000 residential fires are caused by clothes dryers each year — and the primary culprit is a failure to clean lint from the dryer vent system. In Florida's climate, where dryers run frequently year-round, this risk is even more pronounced.

When lint accumulates in your dryer vent, it blocks airflow and causes your dryer to overheat. This not only creates a dangerous fire hazard but also forces your dryer to work harder, dramatically shortening its lifespan and increasing your electricity bills. Most homeowners are unaware that the dryer vent extends far beyond the dryer itself — it runs through walls, floors, or ceilings, often accumulating lint at bends and transitions where it cannot be reached with a standard brush.

Our professional dryer vent cleaning service in Orlando uses commercial rotary brush systems combined with high-powered vacuums to clear the entire length of your dryer duct — from the dryer connection all the way to the exterior exhaust vent. We also inspect the exterior cap to ensure it opens and closes properly and isn't blocked by debris or nesting pests.

Benefits of Professional Dryer Vent Cleaning:

  • Drastically reduces fire risk from lint buildup
  • Clothes dry faster — shorter cycle times
  • Lower energy bills from improved efficiency
  • Extends the life of your dryer
  • Prevents overheating and dryer damage
  • Eliminates musty odors from damp lint
  • Removes pest nesting material from vent
  • Ensures exterior vent cap opens fully

Warning Signs Your Dryer Vent Needs Cleaning:

  • Clothes take more than one cycle to dry fully
  • Dryer feels very hot to the touch during use
  • Burning smell when dryer is running
  • Excessive lint around dryer or laundry room
  • Exterior vent flap not opening when dryer runs
  • More than 12 months since last professional cleaning

HVAC System Cleaning in Orlando, FL

Full heating & cooling system cleaning for maximum efficiency and air quality

Your HVAC system is the central hub of your home's air quality — and it's far more than just the ducts. Dust, biological growth, and debris accumulate on evaporator coils, blower wheels, air handler cabinets, drain pans, and heat exchangers. A dirty HVAC system not only degrades your indoor air quality but also reduces energy efficiency by up to 40%, forces the system to work harder, and dramatically shortens its operational lifespan.

Our full HVAC system cleaning service in Orlando addresses every component of your heating and cooling system. In Florida, where air conditioners run virtually year-round, this maintenance is especially critical. Dirty evaporator coils are one of the most common causes of AC inefficiency in the Orlando area — coil insulation from dust and grime forces the system to run longer cycles to reach your desired temperature, driving up your electricity bills month after month.

Our NADCA-certified technicians are trained to safely clean all system components without damaging sensitive parts. After cleaning, most clients report immediate improvements in airflow, cooling performance, and air quality.

HVAC Components We Clean:

  • Evaporator and condenser coils
  • Blower wheel and motor housing
  • Air handler cabinet interior
  • Drain pan and condensate line
  • Heat exchanger (gas systems)
  • All supply and return plenum boxes
  • Filter housing and filter replacement
  • Accessible electrical components (dusting)

Mold Remediation for Air Ducts in Orlando, FL

Safe detection & elimination of mold in your ductwork with EPA-approved treatments

Orlando's warm, humid climate makes mold growth inside air ducts a serious and widespread problem. When mold establishes itself inside your ductwork, your HVAC system becomes a distribution machine — spreading mold spores throughout every room in your home every time it cycles on. This is a significant health concern, particularly for children, elderly residents, and anyone with asthma, allergies, or a compromised immune system.

Mold inside ducts is not always visible and often goes undetected until symptoms — musty odors, allergy flare-ups, or respiratory irritation — become severe. Our mold remediation service begins with a thorough inspection of your duct system, including camera inspection of areas that are not visible to the naked eye. If mold is confirmed, we perform a complete source removal process before applying EPA-registered antimicrobial treatments to prevent regrowth.

Unlike surface treatments that simply mask mold, our process physically removes mold-contaminated material and applies a protective coating that inhibits future microbial growth. We follow strict NADCA and EPA protocols throughout the remediation process to ensure your home is safe and properly treated.

Our Mold Remediation Process:

  • Full camera inspection to detect mold location
  • Containment setup to prevent cross-contamination
  • Physical removal of mold-contaminated debris
  • HEPA-filtered vacuum cleanup of all particles
  • Application of EPA-registered antimicrobial
  • Anti-microbial fog treatment throughout duct system
  • Post-treatment inspection and documentation
  • Recommendations to prevent future mold growth
