HVAC Cleaning Orlando

Full HVAC System Cleaning in Orlando, FL

In Orlando, your air conditioner runs for 10 to 12 months out of the year. That level of continuous operation means every component of your HVAC system accumulates dirt, dust, biological growth, and debris far faster than in colder climates. The result is reduced efficiency, poor indoor air quality, higher energy bills, and premature system failure — all of which are preventable with regular professional HVAC cleaning.

Air duct cleaning addresses the ductwork. HVAC system cleaning addresses the mechanical components — the evaporator coils, condenser coils, blower wheel, air handler cabinet, drain pan, and condensate line. These components are the heart of your system, and when they are coated with even a thin layer of dust and biofilm, their efficiency drops dramatically.

Dirty evaporator coils are one of the most common reasons Orlando homeowners see their AC bills spike in summer. A coil with even 0.042 inches of fouling loses up to 21% of its heat transfer efficiency. That means your system runs longer cycles, uses more electricity, and still struggles to cool your home. A professional HVAC cleaning restores efficiency, reduces runtime, and can measurably lower your monthly energy costs.

HVAC Components We Clean:

  • Evaporator coils (indoor unit)
  • Blower wheel and motor housing
  • Air handler cabinet interior
  • Drain pan and condensate line
  • All supply and return plenums
  • Filter housing and rack
  • Accessible electrical components
  • Heat exchanger (gas systems)
  • Condenser unit exterior (optional)
  • New filter installation

Our NADCA-certified technicians are trained to clean HVAC components safely without damaging sensitive electronics, coil fins, or refrigerant lines. We use manufacturer-approved cleaning solutions and techniques specifically designed for HVAC equipment. After the cleaning, we test the system to confirm improved airflow and proper operation before we leave.

We recommend full HVAC system cleaning once a year for Orlando homes, ideally before the summer cooling season begins in May. Combined with our air duct cleaning service, this represents the most complete indoor air quality treatment available for your home.
