Dryer Vent Cleaning Orlando

Professional Dryer Vent Cleaning in Orlando, FL

Did you know that clogged dryer vents cause nearly 3,000 house fires every year in the United States? According to the U.S. Fire Administration, clothes dryers are one of the leading causes of residential fires — and the overwhelming majority of those fires start because of a failure to clean lint from the dryer vent system. In Florida's climate, where dryers run nearly year-round, this fire hazard is very real and very preventable.

Most homeowners don't realize that the dryer vent extends far beyond the back of the dryer. It travels through walls, floors, or ceilings, often accumulating dense lint deposits at every bend and transition. A standard household dryer brush cannot reach these deposits. Only professional equipment — specifically commercial rotary brush systems combined with powerful vacuums — can fully clear the entire length of your dryer vent from the machine connection to the exterior cap.

Beyond the fire risk, a clogged dryer vent forces your dryer to work harder and run longer. Clothes that used to dry in one cycle now take two or three. Your dryer's motor and heating element are overworked, dramatically shortening the appliance's lifespan and pushing up your electricity bills month after month. A single professional dryer vent cleaning typically pays for itself within a few months of improved efficiency.

What Our Dryer Vent Cleaning Includes:

  • Full vent line inspection from dryer to cap
  • Commercial rotary brush cleaning system
  • High-powered vacuum debris removal
  • Clearing of all bends and transitions
  • Exterior vent cap inspection and cleaning
  • Check for proper venting angle
  • Bird nest and pest obstruction removal
  • Airflow test before and after service
  • Written service report
  • Safety recommendations if needed

Your Dryer Vent Needs Cleaning If:

  • Clothes take more than one cycle to fully dry
  • Clothes or dryer feel excessively hot after a cycle
  • You smell burning during or after a dryer cycle
  • The exterior vent flap doesn't open while dryer runs
  • More than 12 months since your last professional cleaning
  • Lint is accumulating around the dryer or in the laundry room

We recommend dryer vent cleaning at least once per year for most households, and every 6 months for families that do 5+ loads of laundry per week. Professional dryer vent cleaning is one of the single most impactful home safety maintenance tasks you can perform.

Why Clean Your Dryer Vent?

  • Prevents house fires from lint buildup
  • Clothes dry in one cycle again
  • Reduces electricity bills
  • Extends life of your dryer
  • Eliminates overheating risk
  • Removes pest nests from vent
  • Restores proper exhaust airflow
